Mode: Operate. The owner arrives to verify a large library or review rejected Lidarr imports; the primary action is starting an incremental scan, with full audit deliberately secondary.

Evidence and states: show library totals, verified/skipped/corrupt/warning counts, byte progress, throughput, ETA, current path, validator, live log, cancellation, empty/first-run, offline gate, and completed history. Real paths and results are operational evidence.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm integrity console where the scan itself is the dominant instrument, refusing a generic tile-only admin dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: AudioMuse-derived light/dark neutral surfaces, blue primary actions, compact system typography, softly rounded white/charcoal panels, precise green/amber/red states, and a persistent sidebar.

STORY: Understand library health, launch one safe action, watch bytes become verified evidence, then act outside the app using exact paths.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sidebar at left; title and language/theme controls above a wide live scan panel. Progress, primary incremental action, and four outcome counts remain visible together; recent defects begin below.

FORM: User-pinned AudioMuse operational console, first and binding reference; seed key `user-pinned:audiomuse`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
